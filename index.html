About Arima Bedside Care

Your Trusted Partner in Compassionate Health Support

At Arima Bedside Care, we understand that health journeys require patience, specialized attention, and dignity. Based in Kampala, Uganda, we bridge the gap between hospital-level monitoring and the comfort of your home.

Empathetic Care

We treat every client with empathy, respect, and unconditional kindness, fostering warm companionship alongside personal assistance.

Qualified Caregivers

Our caregivers are professionally trained to handle vital sign checks, wound care management, medication schedules, and daily living needs.

Flexible Coverage

Choose between 12-hour daytime coverage or 24-hour continuous care packages designed around your family’s routine and budget.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Bedside Services

Tailored caregiving solutions to meet individual medical and personal needs.

Bedside Nursing Care

Dedicated personal nursing for hospital inpatients or home-bound individuals requiring vital signs tracking, wound dressing, and routine medication management.

Elderly & Home Care

Support for senior citizens seeking dignity and comfort in their own homes. Includes grooming, feeding, mobility assistance, and companionship.

Post-Surgery & Recovery Care

Specialized post-op care to minimize complications, assist with mobility, monitor healing wounds, and schedule doctor follow-ups.

Palliative & Chronic Care

Gentle, symptom-focused care for patients navigating chronic illness, prioritizing comfort, dignity, pain oversight, and family reassurance.

Pre & Post-Natal Care

Specialized support for expectant and new mothers. Includes maternal vital monitoring, infant care, practical feeding guidance, and vaccination alerts.

Companionship & Daily Living

Emotional support, light household assistance, nutrition planning, and friendly interaction to improve quality of life and mental well-being.
